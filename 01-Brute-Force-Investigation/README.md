# 01 - Brute Force Investigation

## Objective
Investigate a simulated series of failed login attempts and determine whether the activity is suspicious or indicative of a brute-force attack.

## Investigation Workflow
1. Review authentication logs.
2. Identify repeated failed login attempts.
3. Check source IP, username, timestamps, and frequency.
4. Look for successful login after repeated failures.
5. Determine whether the activity is authorized or suspicious.
6. Document findings and response actions.
